\subsection{Unavailable and Unallocated Encodings}
An unallocated encoding or an encoding marked (:term:base.terms.reserved:) does not define alternative
architectural behavior. Instruction (:term:base.terms.opcode|plural:), extension
(:term:base.terms.opcode|plural:), and effective-address forms with that designation, together with encodings
for an optional instruction group whose CPUID feature bit is clear, raise
(:ref:base.events.EXCEPTION.UNAVAILABLE_INSTRUCTION_EXTENSION:) during decode.

RDCR and WRCR use a 16-bit selector and transfer a 64-bit register image. Both instructions are supervisor-only.
Selectors not listed below raise \texttt{INVALID\_CONTROL\_STATE}. Reserved register bits read as zero, and a write
with a nonzero reserved bit raises \texttt{INVALID\_CONTROL\_STATE} without changing the register. The entry,
stack, and user-return registers are per-logical-processor architectural state.

SPC and EPC are exact entry addresses, must be 16-byte aligned, and must be canonical and executable under SCS and
ECS respectively. SSP, ISP, FSP, and DSP are 64-byte-aligned configured stack tops and are not modified by entry or
return. SCS, SDS, ECS, EDS, SSS, ISS, FSS, and DSS contain validated segment images. A write that establishes
URCTL.V validates a user STATUS image with PM, EA, and reserved bits clear; SYSRET revalidates the complete bank.
BOOTPC and BOOTCFG are initialized by the platform during cold reset and preserved by RESET.

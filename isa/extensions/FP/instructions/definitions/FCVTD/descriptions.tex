Converts between the D format in an Fn operand and the representation selected by the suffix. The integer suffixes
are signed B/W/L/Q; \texttt{.H} and \texttt{.S} select binary16 and binary32. For Fn-to-Fn conversion, the source
has the suffix format and the destination has D format. With one Fn and one Rn or memory operand, the Fn has D
format and the other operand has the suffix representation. Floating-format EA sources are memory-only.

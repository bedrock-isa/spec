Examines the selected floating-point encoding without generating a (:term:FP.terms.floating_point_exception_condition:) and writes a one-hot class bitmap to the destination (:term:base.terms.rn_register:).

The destination is a one-hot bitmap selected directly from the sign, exponent, fraction, and NaN quiet bit of the S or D source encoding.

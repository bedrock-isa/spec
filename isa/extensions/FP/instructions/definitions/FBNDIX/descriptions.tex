FBNDIX treats the low bound as inclusive and the high bound as exclusive. It sets (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.V:) when the value is unordered or outside [lo, hi) and clears (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.Z:), (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.N:), and (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.C:). IEEE-754 FFLAGS causes continue to accrue independently.

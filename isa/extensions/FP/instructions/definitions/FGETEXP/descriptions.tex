Derives a signed exponent from the selected source\textquotesingle{}s exponent (:term:base.terms.field:) and converts that integer to the selected floating-point destination format.

For S format, exponent (:term:base.terms.field:) zero is treated as biased exponent one and the bias 127 is subtracted. For D format, exponent (:term:base.terms.field:) zero is likewise treated as one and bias 1023 is subtracted. The resulting signed integer exponent is converted to the selected floating-point destination format. This rule gives zero and subnormal inputs the minimum normal exponent and leaves infinity and NaN encodings at the exponent produced by their all-ones (:term:base.terms.field:).

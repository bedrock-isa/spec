Compares the selected-format operands and writes (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.Z:), (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.N:), (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.C:), and (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.V:) as greater=0000, equal=1000, less=0110, or unordered=0001. A quiet NaN is unordered; a signaling NaN is unordered and generates NV.

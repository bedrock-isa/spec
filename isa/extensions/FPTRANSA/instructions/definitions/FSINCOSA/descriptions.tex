Computes the paired $\sin(x)$ and $\cos(x)$ references in radians over finite $x$ with $|x| \le \pi/4$ after DAZ and writes implementation-bounded S- or D-format approximations under the selected FPTRANSA accuracy contract.

The required FPTRANSA\_ACCURACY contract is \texttt{0x0004}.

\textbf{Special values}\par
\begin{itemize}
\item Matching destination registers: (:ref:base.events.EXCEPTION.INVALID_OPERAND_RELATION:) is raised before operands or floating-point state are read.
\item Infinite source or source magnitude above $\pi/4$: NV is generated and both results are the architectural default quiet NaN.
\item Zero source: the sine result preserves the source zero and the cosine result is positive one.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Exact anchors}\par
\begin{itemize}
\item $\sin(+0) = +0$ and $\cos(+0) = +1$
\item $\sin(-0) = -0$ and $\cos(-0) = +1$
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Required properties}\par
\begin{itemize}
\item the ULP bound applies independently to both results
\item separate FSINA and FCOSA executions may produce different results
\end{itemize}

For nontrapping execution, the sine and cosine destinations are written atomically.

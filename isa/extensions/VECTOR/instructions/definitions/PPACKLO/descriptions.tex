PPACKLO reads the significant predicate positions for the selected W, L, or Q
source element width.  It copies those positions in lane order to the lower
half of the significant destination positions for elements half that width.
The complete destination predicate image is written: the upper half of its
significant positions and every nonsignificant bit are cleared.  The operation
leaves FLAGS unchanged.

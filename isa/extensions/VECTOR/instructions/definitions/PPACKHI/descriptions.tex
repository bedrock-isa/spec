PPACKHI reads the significant predicate positions for the selected W, L, or Q
source element width.  It copies those positions in lane order to the upper
half of the significant destination positions for elements half that width.
The complete destination predicate image is written: the lower half of its
significant positions and every bit outside its significant positions are cleared.  The operation
leaves FLAGS unchanged.

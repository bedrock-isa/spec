PSLIDEUP moves the significant predicate positions for the selected B, W, L, or Q width toward higher typed-lane indices by the unsigned immediate count.
Vacated positions at the low end become zero.
A count at least as large as the architectural typed-lane count clears every significant position.
The complete destination predicate image is written, and every nonsignificant bit is cleared.
The operation leaves FLAGS unchanged.

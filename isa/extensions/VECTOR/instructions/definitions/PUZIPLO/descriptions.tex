PUZIPLO selects the even-position significant predicate lanes for the chosen B, W, L, or Q width.
The selected lanes from the left source form the lower half of the result, and those from the right source form the upper half, with each half retaining source order.
The complete destination predicate image is written, and every bit outside its significant positions is cleared.
The operation leaves FLAGS unchanged.

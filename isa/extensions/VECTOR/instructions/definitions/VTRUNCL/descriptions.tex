VTRUNCL copies the low L (:term:base.terms.field:) of each Q source container into the low L (:term:base.terms.field:) of
the corresponding destination container.  For an active container, the high L
(:term:base.terms.field:) of the destination retains its previous value.  An inactive container
retains its complete previous destination value.  The operation leaves FLAGS
unchanged.

VDUP fills every selected-width destination lane with the same source value.
The integer-register forms use the low B, W, L, or Q (:term:base.terms.field:) of Rn; the
floating-point forms copy the selected H, S, or D value from Fn; and the
immediate forms broadcast their complete encoded B, W, L, or Q value. The
operation replaces the complete destination vector image and leaves FFLAGS
unchanged.

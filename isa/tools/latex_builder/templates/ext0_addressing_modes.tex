\subsection{Extended EA Addressing Modes}\label{section:extended-ea-addressing}
EXT0 is used when compact EA cannot express the operand: explicit segment selection, indexed addressing, zero-base
addressing, SP/PC indexed addressing, or auto-update addressing.

Each EXT0 form selects an encoded SREG SEG(s), fixed SS or CS through SP or PC, or the operation default data
segment.

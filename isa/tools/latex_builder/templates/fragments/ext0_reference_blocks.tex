\textbf{\texttt{EXT0 Explicit Segment Indexed}}\par
\vspace{2pt}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{1.10in}X@{}}
\textbf{Assembler syntax} & [SEG(s):Rn(b) + Rn(i) * scale + displacement], [SEG(s):Rn(b) + Rn(i)++ * scale + displacement], [SEG(s):Rn(b) + {-}{-}Rn(i) * scale + displacement]\\
\textbf{Descriptor} & Byte 0 is \texttt{1sss0010}, \texttt{1sss0000}, or \texttt{1sss0001} for no update, postincrement, or predecrement. Byte 1 is \texttt{bbbbiiii}.\\
\textbf{Generation} & offset = temporary Rn(b) + index\_term * scale + displacement.\\
\textbf{Segment} & SEG(s) selects CS, DS, SS, or GS0..GS4.\\
\textbf{Scale} & Scale is implicit in the consuming instruction. B/W/L/Q normally imply 1/2/4/8.\\
\textbf{Payload} & Two descriptor bytes plus the optional displacement selected by the compact EXT0 escape.\\
\textbf{Update} & Index postincrement uses the current temporary Rn(i), then increments it by one element. Index predecrement decrements temporary Rn(i) by one element before use.\\
\end{tabularx}\par

\textbf{\texttt{EXT0 SP/PC Indexed}}\par
\vspace{2pt}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{1.10in}X@{}}
\textbf{Assembler syntax} & [SP + Rn(i) * scale + displacement], [SP + Rn(i)++ * scale + displacement], [SP + {-}{-}Rn(i) * scale + displacement], and the matching PC forms.\\
\textbf{Descriptor} & Byte 0 is \texttt{10001010} for SP or \texttt{10001011} for PC. Byte 1 is \texttt{0010iiii}, \texttt{0000iiii}, or \texttt{0001iiii} for no update, postincrement, or predecrement.\\
\textbf{Generation} & offset = temporary SP or PC term + index\_term * scale + displacement.\\
\textbf{Segment} & SP forms are fixed to SS. PC forms are fixed to CS.\\
\textbf{Payload} & Two descriptor bytes plus the optional displacement selected by the compact EXT0 escape.\\
\textbf{Update} & Only the Rn(i) index register may auto-update; SP and PC are not auto-updated by these forms.\\

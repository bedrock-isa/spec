\manualinstructiondescriptionheading{Performance-Counter IDs}
RDPMC selects a 64-bit counter with its 16-bit immediate ID. The three standard counters are mandatory and wrap
modulo $2^{64}$. Cold reset and warm RESET clear them to zero. PMC.EN controls whether they increment.

\manualtablecaption{Standard Performance Counters}
\begin{manualtabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.55in}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{1.05in}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.80in}@{}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{ManualHeaderFill}
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Name} & \textbf{Increment event}\\
\midrule
\texttt{0} & \texttt{CYCLE} & each implementation cycle while PMC.EN is set\\
\texttt{1} & \texttt{INSTRET} & each successfully committed architectural instruction\\
\texttt{2} & \texttt{PTWALK} & start of a hardware page walk after a paging-enabled TLB miss\\
\bottomrule
\end{manualtabular}

Each successfully committed repeat-body iteration contributes one INSTRET event. A faulting instruction does not.
PTWALK includes a walk that later reports a page fault, but excludes a segment-bound or canonical-address failure
detected before a walk begins. IDs beyond the standard set are implementation-defined and are discovered through the
CPUID performance-counter leaf. Reading an unavailable ID raises \texttt{INVALID\_CONTROL\_STATE}.

\begin{manualraggedblock}
Supervisor mode may read every implemented counter. User-mode RDPMC requires PMC.USER; otherwise it raises
\texttt{PRIVILEGE\_FAULT}. Clearing PMC.EN freezes the current counter values without clearing them.
\end{manualraggedblock}

\subsection{Control-Register Selector Namespace}
RDCR and WRCR use a 16-bit selector and transfer a 64-bit register image. Both instructions are supervisor-only.
Selectors not listed below raise \texttt{INVALID\_CONTROL\_STATE}. Reserved register bits read as zero, and a write
with a nonzero reserved bit raises \texttt{INVALID\_CONTROL\_STATE} without changing the register.

\begin{manualtabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.75in}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{1.05in}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.55in}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Selector} & \textbf{Register} & \textbf{Use}\\
\midrule
\texttt{0x0000} & \texttt{PTCR} & page-table root and translation control\\
\texttt{0x0001} & \texttt{ASCR} & address-space identifier control\\
\texttt{0x0002} & \texttt{ICR} & interrupt-vector and delivery control\\
\texttt{0x0100} & \texttt{SPC} & SYSCALL supervisor-entry program counter\\
\texttt{0x0101} & \texttt{SCS} & SYSCALL supervisor-entry code-segment image\\
\texttt{0x0102} & \texttt{SDS} & SYSCALL supervisor-entry data-segment image\\
\texttt{0x0200} & \texttt{SSS0} & supervisor stack-segment image for stack set 0\\
\texttt{0x0201} & \texttt{SSP0} & supervisor stack pointer for stack set 0\\
\texttt{0x0210} & \texttt{SSS1} & supervisor stack-segment image for stack set 1\\
\texttt{0x0211} & \texttt{SSP1} & supervisor stack pointer for stack set 1\\
\texttt{0x0220} & \texttt{SSS2} & supervisor stack-segment image for stack set 2\\
\texttt{0x0221} & \texttt{SSP2} & supervisor stack pointer for stack set 2\\
\texttt{0x0230} & \texttt{SSS3} & supervisor stack-segment image for stack set 3\\
\texttt{0x0231} & \texttt{SSP3} & supervisor stack pointer for stack set 3\\
\texttt{0x1000} & \texttt{BOOTPC} & warm-reset target supplied by the platform at cold reset\\
\texttt{0x1001} & \texttt{BOOTCFG} & opaque platform boot configuration preserved by warm reset\\
\texttt{0x1100} & \texttt{PMC} & performance-monitor enable and user-access control\\
\bottomrule
\end{manualtabular}

SPC is consumed according to the SYSCALL entry rules. SCS, SDS, and each SSSn register contain validated 64-bit
segment images. Each SSPn register contains the raw 64-bit stack pointer used for the selected supervisor stack set.
BOOTPC and BOOTCFG are initialized by the platform during cold reset and preserved by the RESET instruction.

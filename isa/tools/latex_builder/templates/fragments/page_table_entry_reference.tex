\subsection{Page-Table Entry Format}

Every page-table entry is 64 bits. The low twelve bits contain hardware-defined traversal, permission, and memory
attributes. The physical frame number occupies bits \texttt{PABITS-1..12}; bits above the selected physical-address
width are available to software and ignored by the page-table walker.

\texttt{G} & \texttt{4} & global translation\\
\texttt{A} & \texttt{5} & accessed; set on successful traversal or leaf use\\
\texttt{D} & \texttt{6} & dirty; leaf-only and set on a successful store\\
\texttt{AT} & \texttt{7} & 0 byte-addressed; 1 externally acknowledged / bus-sized leaf access\\
\texttt{CP} & \texttt{9..8} & 0 cacheable; 1 uncacheable; 2 write-through; 3 write-combining\\
\texttt{SW0} & \texttt{10} & software-defined and ignored by hardware\\
\texttt{T} & \texttt{11} & one for a non-leaf table pointer; zero for an L1 leaf\\
\texttt{PFN} & \texttt{PABITS-1..12} & next-table or mapped-page physical frame number\\
software & \texttt{63..PABITS} & software-defined and ignored by hardware\\
\bottomrule
\end{manualtabular}

At every level above L1, P and T must both be one; D, G, and AT must be zero. At L1, P must be one and T must be
zero. W, X, and U permissions are accumulated across the walk. A reserved or malformed consumed entry raises
\texttt{PAGE\_FAULT}. Supervisor current-domain access to a user mapping faults; the checked user-domain operations
select the user-domain permission path explicitly.

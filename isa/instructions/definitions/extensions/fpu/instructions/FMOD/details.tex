After DAZ processing, let \(x\) be the original destination and \(y\) the source. For finite valid operands, choose \(q\) as \(x/y\) truncated toward zero and compute
\[
  r = x - qy
\]
with exact intermediate arithmetic. A zero result has the sign of \(x\).

A signaling NaN generates NV; a NaN input produces the selected quiet NaN. Infinite \(x\) or zero \(y\) generates NV and produces the architectural default quiet NaN. Infinite \(y\) with finite \(x\) returns \(x\). An enabled floating-point exception condition raises the \texttt{FLOATING\_POINT\_FAULT} architectural event before the destination write.

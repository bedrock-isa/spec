WRSTATUS validates the low 16 bits from the source (:term:base.terms.rn_register:) and atomically updates (:ref:base.registers.STATE.STATUS.IE:), (:ref:base.registers.STATE.STATUS.NI:), (:ref:base.registers.STATE.STATUS.TF:), and (:ref:base.registers.STATE.STATUS.RF:). Reserved bits (:term:base.terms.must_be_zero:), and the supplied (:ref:base.registers.STATE.STATUS.PM:), (:ref:base.registers.STATE.STATUS.EA:), (:ref:base.registers.STATE.STATUS.EDEPTH:), and (:ref:base.registers.STATE.STATUS.UO:) values must equal the current values. Clearing (:ref:base.registers.STATE.STATUS.NI:) with a pending NMI admits that NMI at the next instruction boundary.

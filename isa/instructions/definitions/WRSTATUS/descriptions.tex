WRSTATUS validates the low 16 bits from the source (:term:base.terms.rn_register:) and atomically updates IE, NI, TF, and RF. Reserved bits (:term:base.terms.must_be_zero:), and the supplied PM, EA, EDEPTH, and UO values must equal the current values. Clearing NI with a pending NMI admits that NMI at the next instruction boundary.

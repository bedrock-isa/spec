RESET serializes earlier (:term:base.terms.memory_effect|plural:), applies the complete architectural warm-reset state to the executing (:term:base.terms.logical_processor:), and resumes execution at BOOTPC.

RESET is supervisor-only and affects only the executing (:term:base.terms.logical_processor:). Before the reset-state transition commits,
all earlier (:term:base.terms.memory_effect|plural:) and pending write-combining stores from that (:term:base.terms.logical_processor:) complete. No younger
instruction effect becomes visible.

The complete register, hidden-state, and cache effects of the transition are defined under
\hyperref[section:event-reset-and-context-state]{Event Reset and Context State}. Instruction-fetch synchronization
completes before the first instruction at BOOTPC is fetched.

Tests the destination bit selected by the low six bits of the bit index and writes (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.Z:) from whether that bit was clear while clearing (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.N:), (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.C:), and (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.V:), without changing the destination. The B-sized 0 or 1 value of the old tested bit supplies the REPcc observed value.

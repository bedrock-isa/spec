BNDSII treats both bounds as inclusive. It sets (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.V:) when signed(value) is outside [signed(lo), signed(hi)] and clears (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.Z:), (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.N:), and (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.C:). The B-sized 0 or 1 violation result supplies the REPcc observed value.

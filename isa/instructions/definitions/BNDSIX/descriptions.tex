BNDSIX treats the low bound as inclusive and the high bound as exclusive. It sets (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.V:) when signed(value) is outside [signed(lo), signed(hi)) and clears (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.Z:), (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.N:), and (:ref:base.registers.STATE.FLAGS.C:). The B-sized 0 or 1 violation result supplies the REPcc observed value.

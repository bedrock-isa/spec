When the implementation trace stream is enabled, appends the low 16-bit marker and current instruction boundary to that stream; otherwise it completes without changing (:term:base.terms.architectural_state:). The tracing terminology distinguishes this marker instruction from a TF-selected (:term:base.terms.trace_unit:) and the (:ref:base.events.EXCEPTION.SINGLE_STEP:) architectural event. RF does not suppress the marker.

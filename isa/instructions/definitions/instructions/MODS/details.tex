Let \(n\) be the selected operand width, \(d\) the signed two\textquotesingle{}s-complement dividend, and \(s\) the signed two\textquotesingle{}s-complement divisor. The quotient and remainder are defined by
\[
  q = \operatorname{trunc}(d/s),
  \qquad r = d - qs .
\]
Thus a nonzero remainder has the sign of the dividend. The destination supplies the dividend and receives \(r\).

A zero divisor and the most-negative value divided by \(-1\) raise \texttt{DIVIDE\_ERROR}; no destination is changed.

The source address is checked for canonicality before the page-table walk. The walk begins at the PTCR root and follows the active LA48 or LA57 hierarchy using the virtual page number.

Every continuing entry must be a valid table pointer. U, W, and X are accumulated across the walk. A valid leaf at level $L$ terminates the walk and combines its naturally aligned physical base with the original low $12+9(L-1)$ address bits to form a byte address. PTE bit 7 is ignored. Success sets Z and reports effective U, W, and X in N, C, and V. Any canonicality or walk failure returns zero and clears all four flags without raising \texttt{PAGE\_FAULT}, setting PTE A or D, or modifying the TLB.

R0 supplies a 32-bit form selector and a 32-bit control word; R1 through R3 supply the operand descriptors. Small literals and payload-width choices are carried directly in the control word, while larger immediates, displacements, and extended effective-address descriptors are referenced by descriptor payload mode.

The instruction decodes one semantic descriptor, validates its mnemonic, extension availability, size, condition, operands, payload widths, aliases, and addressing choices, and selects the shortest canonical encoding. Descriptor failure writes the defined negative status to R0 and leaves memory unchanged. On success, the complete destination range is validated before the encoded byte sequence is stored as one fault-atomic update, after which R0 receives the byte count.
